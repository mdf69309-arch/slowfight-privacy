Slow Fight

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 20, 2026

The short version

  • Slow Fight collects no personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
  • Your score, coins and settings stay on your phone.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

The sections below explain each point in detail.

Who we are

Slow Fight (“the game”) is an Android game published by AD_oisal Ahmed (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, write to us at md69309@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal detail. It has no sign-in, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not send information to servers run by us.

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps a small amount of data in its private storage on your phone:

High score
Your best score, shown on the main screen.
Coin balance
The coins you have collected or bought.
Planes
Which planes you have unlocked and which one you selected.
Sound settings
Whether background music and sound effects are on.
Purchase records
Identifiers of completed purchases, kept only so the same purchase is never credited twice.

This data never leaves your device. Other apps cannot read it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

In-app purchases

Coin packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Payment happens inside Google Play, so your card number, payment method and billing details go to Google and are never visible to the game or to us.

Google provides us with standard sales records in Play Console, such as the order number, the product, the amount, and the country and date of purchase. We use these only for accounting, refunds and customer support. How Google handles your payment information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Third-party services

The only outside service the game works with is Google Play, through the Google Play Billing Library. We do not use advertising networks, analytics providers or social media tools, and we do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

Permissions

The game does not request any runtime permissions. It declares the standard Google Play billing permission so that in-app purchases work.

Children

Slow Fight is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Security and retention

Because we do not collect personal information, we hold nothing about you on our side. Data on your phone is protected by Android’s app sandbox.

Your choices

  • To remove all game data, uninstall the game or open Settings, then Apps, then Slow Fight, then Storage, and choose Clear storage. This also resets your coins and unlocked planes, and we cannot restore them.
  • To review or ask for a refund on a purchase, use Google Play’s order history or contact Google Play support.
  • Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You are still welcome to write to us with any request.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, for example by adding online features, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the change goes live. The same text is available inside the game under Privacy.
