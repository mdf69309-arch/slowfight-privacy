Slow Fight

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

The short version

  • Slow Fight uses Google Sign-In so your progress can follow you across devices.
  • We store your coin balance, high score and purchase history under your account.
  • We collect only your Google email, name and profile photo — nothing more.
  • There is no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDK.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

The sections below explain each point in detail.

Who we are

Slow Fight (“the game”) is an Android game published by AD_ Foisal Ahmed (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, write to us at mdf69309@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store the following basic profile information:

  • Your email address
  • Your display name
  • Your profile photo URL

We do not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, or any other Google account data beyond the basic sign-in profile above. The game contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting trackers.

What is stored on your device

The game also keeps a small amount of data in its private local storage on your phone, so it works even offline:

High score
Your best score, shown on the main screen.
Coin balance
The coins you have collected or bought.
Planes
Which planes you have unlocked and which one you selected.
Sound settings
Whether background music and sound effects are on.
Purchase records
Identifiers of completed purchases, kept only so the same purchase is never credited twice.

This local copy is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

What is stored in the cloud

Once you sign in, the following is also saved to our Firebase account, linked to your Google account:

  • Your email address, name and profile photo (shown on your in-game profile screen)
  • Your coin balance and high score, so progress carries over between devices
  • A history of completed coin purchases, including the transaction / order ID, product, price and date, shown to you under Profile → Purchase History

This data is stored securely with Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) and is only ever readable by your own signed-in account.

In-app purchases

Coin packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Payment happens inside Google Play, so your card number, payment method and billing details go to Google and are never visible to the game or to us.

Google provides us with standard sales records in Play Console, such as the order number, the product, the amount, and the country and date of purchase. A copy of the order ID, product and price is also saved to your in-game purchase history as described above. We use this data only for accounting, refunds and customer support. How Google handles your payment information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Third-party services

The game uses the following Google services to operate:

  • Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication — to sign you in
  • Firebase Realtime Database — to store your coins, high score and purchase history
  • Google Play Billing — to process in-app purchases

We do not use advertising networks, third-party analytics or social media SDKs, and we do not sell or share your personal information with anyone outside of the Google services listed above. See the Google Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles this data.

Permissions

The game requests internet access so it can sign you in and sync your progress, and it declares the standard Google Play billing permission so that in-app purchases work. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

Children

Slow Fight is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account or sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Security and retention

Your cloud data is stored using Google Firebase and protected by Firebase security rules, so only your own signed-in account can read or write it. We keep your account data for as long as you use the game, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

  • To delete your cloud data (email, name, photo, coins, high score and purchase history), email us at mdf69309@gmail.com with the email address you signed in with, and we will delete it.
  • To remove local data on this device, open Settings, then Apps, then Slow Fight, then Storage, and choose Clear storage, or uninstall the game. This does not delete your cloud data.
  • To review or ask for a refund on a purchase, use Google Play’s order history or contact Google Play support.
  • You can stop using the game and revoke Slow Fight’s access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the change goes live. The same text is available inside the game under Privacy.
