Delete your account and data

Last updated October 21, 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Open your email app and sign in with any account you like. You do not need to be signed in to the game.
  2. Write to slowfightsupport@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Slow Fight account.
  3. In the message, give the Google email address you use to sign in to Slow Fight. We can only delete an account we can match to that address.
  4. We confirm by email once everything is deleted.

We delete the account and the data listed below within 30 days of receiving your request.

What we delete

When your request is processed, we permanently remove the following from our Firebase project:

  • Your sign-in record (Firebase Authentication)
  • Your email address, display name and profile photo link
  • Your coin balance and high score
  • Your purchase history inside the game, including the copy kept for the purchase index

After deletion, the account cannot be restored. Coins and unlocked planes tied to it are lost, and any unused coins have no cash value.

What we keep

  • Google Play sales records. Google keeps its own order records for purchases made through Google Play (order number, amount, date, country). Google controls these records, and we only use them for accounting and refunds. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Data on your phone. The game also stores your high score, coins and settings on your device. Open Settings, then Apps, then Slow Fight, then Storage, and choose Clear storage, or uninstall the game, to remove it.

We keep nothing else about your account after deletion, and there is no extra retention period.

Stop sharing with Slow Fight without deleting

You can remove Slow Fight’s access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account settings, under Security and then Third-party apps & services. This stops future sign-in, but does not delete data we already hold. For that, send the deletion request above.
